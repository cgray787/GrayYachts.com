# Monaco Yacht Show 2026: day-two news and the questions behind the launches

Local review draft · September 24, 2026 · Seven photographs in the companion HTML.

Monaco Yacht Show 2026 is underway at Port Hercule from September 23 to 26. This September 24 briefing follows the organizer’s opening-day Blue Wake awards announcement and the current press agenda. The useful questions for an owner concern the evidence behind a new system, what a builder has actually announced and whether a design fits the way you use a boat. Reporting here is based on published sources; Gray Yachts is not reporting from the docks.

## Opening-day news: the Blue Wake awards

The organizer’s September 23 release confirms that six Blue Wake awards were presented on the show’s opening day. It names Tykun, Bespoke Projects, Termodinamica, Amico & Co and Marina Ibiza as category winners, with a special yacht prize for Simena by Ares Yachts. The programme works with Water Revolution Foundation to assess the submitted environmental evidence.

The winning subjects reach beyond propulsion. They include tender construction, interior materials, air conditioning, refit facilities and seagrass restoration. That range gives owners several different lines of inquiry. Before applying an award announcement to your own boat, ask which product or project was assessed and whether the operating conditions resemble yours.

## September 24: announcements to follow up

The show’s press agenda lists an Oyster flagship announcement, a Camper & Nicholsons design project and the GX42 world premiere for September 24. It also lists a Romeo Marine presentation covering the completed Romeo 55 and a preview of the Romeo 60 under construction. Those are scheduled presentations; a timetable alone does not establish what was ultimately revealed.

For a buyer, the next useful document is the builder’s dated release or specification sheet. Ask whether the announcement concerns a delivered boat, an available hull, a concept or a future production model. Keep that distinction in your comparison notes, especially if your next purchase depends on a delivery date.

## How to read an environmental claim

Begin with the exact claim. Does the evidence cover manufacturing, operation or the vessel’s full life cycle? Ask what the comparison boat or system was, which assumptions were used and whether the assessment is available to read. Different boundaries can make two impressive figures unsuitable for direct comparison.

Then bring the question back to your own use. A boat that spends long periods at anchor presents a different set of questions from one used for frequent passages. Ask the builder to explain the equipment in that context, including support and service arrangements. A qualified surveyor or marine specialist can help assess whether the proposed system suits the vessel and your plans.

## Use the remaining show days for specific questions

The 2026 event is scheduled to finish on September 26. If you are following remotely, save the announcement you are interested in and note its date. If you are attending, confirm appointments and access directly with the exhibitor before planning around a particular yacht.

A short written brief helps keep the conversation focused. Describe how you intend to use the boat, where you expect to keep it and what you need to understand before a second conversation. Ask for a follow-up document that answers those points. A rendering or launch photograph can introduce a design; plans and verified specifications make it possible to compare alternatives.
