# I need to sell my boat in Seattle. Where should I start?
Target: `/insights/sell-my-boat-seattle` | Primary query: sell my boat Seattle
Description: A practical starting point for selling a boat in Seattle: organize the records, assess condition, compare the market and discuss a valuation with Connor Gray.

## Start with the boat you actually have

To sell a boat in Seattle, first assemble its specifications and service records, document its current condition, and compare it with genuinely similar boats. Then decide on an asking price and a plan for showings. Connor Gray at Gray Yachts works with yacht buyers and sellers in the Pacific Northwest.

A model name and engine hours are a starting point. The useful story includes the work completed, the work still needed, how the boat has been used, and what a buyer will receive. If you are also shopping for your next boat, make that timing part of the first conversation.

## Put the records in one place

Start with the information you can substantiate. Keep unresolved discrepancies visible so they can be checked before the listing goes out. A listing is easier to assess when its photographs, written specifications and records describe the same vessel.

* Builder, model, year, hull identification and current location.
* Engine and generator hours with the date recorded.
* Service invoices, equipment updates and any available survey reports.
* A written list of known defects and equipment included in the sale.
* Ownership paperwork to review with the professionals handling your transaction.
## Ask what supports the proposed asking price

A boat advertised at a particular price has not necessarily sold for that amount. Ask which comparisons are active listings and which are verified completed sales. Record their dates, location, equipment and condition differences.

A newer engine, a recent refit or an unusual layout can change the comparison. Those differences should be explained individually. Avoid treating the most expensive advertised example as proof of your own boat’s value.

## Prepare for the questions a serious buyer will ask

Clean the boat, remove distracting personal items and make machinery spaces accessible. Photograph the current condition, including details that help someone decide whether a visit is worthwhile. Keep a short list of the repairs you intend to finish before showings.

Your marketing plan should make it easy for a buyer to understand the layout, equipment and location. Ask how inquiries will be handled, who will conduct showings, and how feedback will inform a change in presentation or price.

## Coordinate the sale with your next purchase

A new boat debut can be a good reason to reconsider what you want from boating. It does not establish the value of your existing boat. Keep your sale estimate separate from the delivery schedule and terms of a replacement.

If a show has caught your attention, bring the exact model and configuration to the discussion. That makes it possible to compare your current boat, a brokerage alternative and a new build on the same practical questions.

## Talk through a valuation with Connor Gray

Gray Yachts offers a seller questionnaire to begin a Pacific Northwest market valuation. Start with the boat’s details and your preferred timing, then discuss the comparisons and preparation work that would support a listing.

Source: [Gray Yachts seller page](https://grayyachts.com/sell)
### Can I sell before choosing my next boat?
Yes. Discuss your likely selling timetable and your next purchase separately. An announced delivery date should be confirmed before you build a sale plan around it.

### Does a boat show tell me what my boat is worth?
No. A show introduces alternatives, but a valuation requires relevant market comparisons and the condition of your particular vessel.

### How much does it cost to use a yacht broker?
Ask for the proposed agreement and a clear explanation of commission and other potential costs. Do not assume a percentage or expense applies until the terms are confirmed.

Source: [Monaco show dates](https://mediakit.monacoyachtshow.com/)
Source: [Monaco official press releases](https://www.monacoyachtshow.com/en/press-releases)
## Read a sustainability announcement as a starting point

An award or a new propulsion concept gives an owner something specific to investigate. It is not a substitute for understanding how a boat will operate on an actual passage.

For a hybrid or electric system, ask what the published operating figures include. A quiet evening at anchor and a long passage at cruising speed are different energy demands. Ask for a written explanation of the conditions behind any range or consumption claim.

* Which loads are included in the quoted range?
* What speed, weather and reserve assumptions were used?
* Who services the propulsion and battery equipment near the intended home port?
* Which components are supported by separate warranties?
## The new fleet deserves model by model scrutiny

Monaco’s first official 2026 fleet announcement included the Sirena 118 among its new deliveries. For an owner following that brand, the useful next step is to study the precise boat and its documentation. A larger flagship does not by itself tell you how a smaller model will suit your cruising.

## What a Pacific Northwest seller can take from Monaco

If Monaco has put a replacement yacht on your shortlist, document those requirements before changing the price of the boat you already own. The existing boat still needs its own market comparisons, inspection history and selling plan.

# Cannes 2026: Aquila and Windelo give catamaran buyers new questions
Target: `/insights/cannes-2026-catamaran-announcements` | Primary query: Cannes Yachting Festival 2026 new catamarans
Description: Cannes announced Aquila 45 Sail and Windelo 55 Sport premieres. We look at the ownership questions behind the new catamaran coverage.

## What the organizer announced

The Cannes Yachting Festival ran September 8 through 13, 2026. Its August 25 sailing preview named the Aquila 45 Sail and Windelo 55 Sport among the models announced as world premieres. That is the basis for this coverage, rather than a Gray Yachts sea trial.

Source: [Cannes official sailing premiere preview](https://www.cannesyachtingfestival.com/en-gb/le-mag/boating-guide/sailing-boats/inspiring-new-boats-to-change-your-horizon-focus-on-10-world-premiers.html)
## Aquila: compare the sailboat on its own terms

Aquila’s manufacturer site lists the 45 Sail alongside a new 50 Sail and its established power catamaran categories. The wider lineup creates more options to investigate, but a move from power to sail needs its own requirements list.

Source: [Aquila manufacturer model range](https://www.aquilaboats.com/)
## The practical power to sail discussion

Ask who will handle the sails, how the helm works with the cockpit, and which tasks are comfortable with the people normally aboard. Consider where the boat will spend its time and how often you expect to motor.

On a viewing, walk through an ordinary day. Where does wet gear go? Can someone rest while another person cooks? How do guests move around the boat while the crew is busy? Those questions produce a more useful comparison than counting attractive spaces.

## Windelo: ask for the energy plan

Windelo presents electric propulsion and a protected forward cockpit as central parts of its design. Buyers should ask the builder to explain the installed energy system for the exact configuration being considered.

Source: [Windelo manufacturer model range](https://windelo-catamaran.com/en/)
## Separate the design promise from the passage plan

Write down a representative route, the expected time under power and the equipment you would use aboard. Ask how those loads affect the available reserve. For a boat coming to the Pacific Northwest, also ask how charging and service will be arranged.

This is a request for specific operating evidence, not a judgment about a system based on its label. An electric propulsion claim should be explained with the same care as any conventional range or consumption figure.

## Selling before changing categories

If a catamaran has changed your plans, begin the current boat’s sale discussion early. Its condition and relevant market comparisons still determine the valuation work. Gray Yachts can help connect that conversation with a clearer brief for the next boat.

### Did Gray Yachts test these boats at Cannes?
No. The article distinguishes organizer announcements and manufacturer information from an independent inspection or sea trial.

### Do electric propulsion figures apply to every trip?
Ask for the conditions and loads behind the quoted figure. Your route, speed, reserve and onboard use need to be considered together.
