## Decisions for future articles

* The opening must tell the reader something verified or answer the stated question. Avoid opening every show article with the same selling advice.
* A new article needs a distinct event, verified development or owner question. Update an existing article when intent overlaps.
* Put the announcement date beside the event date. Rechecking a source is not a reason to change an article's publication date.
* Do not claim “breaking” when the source is undated or more than 72 hours old.
* Add photographs only with permission and accurate captions. A generic yacht photo is not evidence that Connor attended a show or that a particular model was present.
* Use a genuine broker comment or an inspection note when Connor provides it; never manufacture either.
